## The organization (from mercator-ocean.eu, read 2026-07-31)

- Mercator Ocean International: non-profit organization transforming into an intergovernmental body, dedicated to providing digital Ocean systems and science-driven information services about the state of the Ocean
- Director-General: Pierre Bahurel. Team of 120 experts. HQ: Toulouse, France
- 30 years of operational oceanography (site runs a 30-Year Anniversary section)
- Operates the Copernicus Marine Service for the European Union
- Copernicus Marine Service: free, open-access ocean data; over 1.5 million users worldwide; 85,000 subscribers and nearly 750,000 users as of 2024
- Co-develops / coordinates the European Digital Twin of the Ocean (EDITO) on behalf of the European Commission; "what now / what next / what if" capability layers
- Hosts the OceanPrediction Decade Collaborative Centre under IOC-UNESCO
- Leads GEO Blue Planet and G7 FSOI Coordination Center project offices
- Operates under mandates from the European Union and the United Nations
- 10 public shareholders: CMCC (IT), CNR (IT), CNRS (FR), Ifremer (FR), IRD (FR), Meteo-France (FR), Met Office (UK), NERSC (NO), Puertos del Estado (ES), SHOM (FR)
- Feb 2022 One Ocean Summit (Brest): six European states (FR, IT, NO, PT, ES, UK) committed to transforming Mercator Ocean into an intergovernmental body
- June 2025 UN Ocean Conference (Nice, France): twelve European countries endorsed a joint Declaration committing to the creation of the Mercator International Centre for the Ocean
- VERIFIED on their homepage flags section: **Belgium is among the endorsing countries displayed** (flags shown: Belgium, Estonia, France, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Malta, Norway, Portugal, + 2 more off-screen) -- legitimate relevance angle for a Belgian supplier
- Three pillars: Ocean Data, Ocean Community, Ocean Intelligence
- Key tech named on site: GLORYS12 global ocean reanalysis, GLONET AI forecasting system, hybrid physics + ML modelling, HPC and cloud infrastructures
- Partners named: ESA, EUMETSAT, EuroGoos, EMODnet; DITTO Programme (UN Ocean Decade)
- Site is bilingual EN/FR via WPML (wp-wpml_current_language cookie; /fr/ URL prefix)
- Real homepage H1: "Digital Ocean Knowledge Accessible to All"
- Site sections: About Us, Ocean Data, Ocean Community, Ocean Intelligence, News & Events, Media Centre
- Current websites named in the tender description (per amendment/aggregator data): www.mercator-ocean.eu (multilingual corporate site) and the intranet
