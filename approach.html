Why this concept exists

Demonstration over description.

The award criteria weight technical value at 70 percent. At that weighting a claim of capability is worth little; evidence is worth more. So instead of describing what we would do, this annex does it.

Real measurements, not promises.

The audit page contains findings measured on the live mercator-ocean.eu homepage on 31 July 2026: transfer weight, stylesheet count, missing metadata, absent hreflang. Each finding carries its evidence and its remedy. The method is on display before the contract exists.

Delivery timeline

From award to handover.

All three lots start on 21 September 2026 and run for 24 months. The sequence below assumes all three are awarded together; each lot also stands alone.

  • Weeks 1-2

    Onboarding and access

    Credentials, analytics and Search Console access, repository and hosting handover, intranet access, a shared issue tracker and a kickoff with the editorial team. Nothing ships before access is verified end to end.

  • First quarter

    Lot 1: audit fieldwork and delivery

    The full audit programme runs early, while its findings can still steer everything else: technical crawl, field performance data, log analysis, bilingual keyword mapping, accessibility review and the prioritised roadmap, presented in person or by call.

  • Months 1-24

    Lot 2: a continuous cadence

    Development and technical maintenance of the corporate site and the intranet as a steady rhythm across the full 24 months: planned releases, security updates, editor support and a monthly written status note. No long silences between invoices.

  • First quarter, then continuous

    Lot 3: pipeline live, then operated

    The automatic English/French translation system is implemented and switched on within the first quarter, then operated continuously for the remainder of the contract: glossary maintenance, quality sampling and hreflang integrity checks.

  • Month 24

    Re-measurement and handover

    The audit's own metrics are re-run against the live estate so the work is accountable to its numbers, and handover documentation is delivered so that nothing depends on us to keep running.

Who we are

A small senior studio.

Midnight Space is a Belgian web studio led by its founder, Jordan Van Der Sloten. It is small by design: every deliverable in this tender is built and reviewed by the person who signs the offer. There is no sales layer between the promise and the work.

Standards

The same bar on everything.

These apply to every deliverable in every lot, and this concept site is built to them.

  • WCAG 2.1 AA as an acceptance criterion on every template: skip links, focus states, semantic landmarks, contrast, reduced-motion support. Not an audit afterthought.
  • Performance budgets agreed up front for page weight and Core Web Vitals, enforced in the build so regressions are caught before they are published.
  • GDPR-conscious analytics: consent-aware measurement that collects the minimum needed to answer real editorial and product questions.
  • Everything documented: decisions recorded, runbooks for the intranet and the translation pipeline, and a written status note every month.
  • Owned by the client: code, content, configuration and accounts belong to Mercator Ocean from day one. No supplier lock-in, by contract and by architecture.
